Any questions?

Which watches work?

Wear OS watches such as Samsung Galaxy Watch and Google Pixel Watch. An Apple Watch version is planned.

Do I need my phone on the field?

No. YARD50 runs on the watch alone for the whole game.

Does it follow flag and tackle football rules?

Yes, both. Pick your format before kickoff and set everything: period length, play clock, QB count, number of downs, running or stopped clock, mercy rule. Formats are updated whenever rulebooks change, every season or mid-season depending on the competition.

How much does it cost?

The beta is free. At launch: free for a quick game, Pro at €3.99 a month or €24.99 a year.

What about my data?

It stays on your watch. No account, no ads.
